Break an AI agent. Watch the gate hold.

Prompt injection can't be fully prevented in today's models, so the question that matters is what an injected instruction is allowed to do. Compose an attack, choose a tool for the agent to call, and a provenance-aware gate decides in real time — then maps the decision to MITRE ATLAS and the OWASP LLM Top 10. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

How one decision is made

Detectors catch the obvious attacks, but detectors miss things. The real control is provenance: if untrusted content sits anywhere in the agent's context, nothing with side effects runs without a human — even when the pattern detector is fooled.
