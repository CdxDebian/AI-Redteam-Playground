How one decision is made

The trick isn't detecting every injection — detectors always miss some. It's provenance: if untrusted content is anywhere in the agent's context, nothing with side effects runs without a human. That one rule holds even when the text-pattern detector is fooled.

Untrusted input→ detectors: injection · secrets · egress→ policy gate R0–R6→ ALLOW/ REQUIRE_APPROVAL/ BLOCK
The 7 rules, in plain English
RuleFires whenDecision
R0Tool isn't declared in policyBLOCK (default deny)
R1A secret (API key, token) is in the argumentsBLOCK
R2Destination is a private/metadata IP or a non-allowlisted hostBLOCK
R3Injection markers + a side-effecting toolBLOCK (read-only → allow + alert)
R4Any untrusted content + a side-effecting toolREQUIRE_APPROVAL
R5A high-impact / destructive / persistence toolREQUIRE_APPROVAL
R6Trusted context, nothing flaggedALLOW
